Zamassss – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: Zamassss does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

1. Who we are

Zamassss is a mobile arcade game for iPhone developed by Keigo Moriyama (“we”, “us”).

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account, does not connect to our servers, and does not send any data off your device.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves a few settings and scores locally on your iPhone only:

  • your high scores;
  • which Zamass voices you have unlocked;
  • whether the menu music is on or off.

This information never leaves your device. If you delete the app, it is deleted with it.

4. Third parties

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools from third parties, and we do not share data with anyone.

5. Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal data. It may use your iPhone’s motion sensor (to detect a shake in “Shake the Sunset”) and haptic feedback (vibration). Motion data is used only in the moment, on your device, and is never saved or sent anywhere.

6. Children’s privacy

Because we collect no personal data from anyone, the app is safe for children of all ages to use from a privacy standpoint.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us at keigo.moriyama@gmail.com.
